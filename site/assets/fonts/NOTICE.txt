saira-cond-700-latin.woff2 and spline-mono-latin.woff2 are the Latin subsets of the open fonts Saira Condensed and
Spline Sans Mono (Google Fonts), licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org). Saira
Condensed is used for large headings and small labels, Spline Sans Mono for code. They are served from this site
only; the pages load nothing from a font service. Body text uses the system font.
