The logo_short_*.svg and logo_long_*.svg files in this folder are the official King of Gores artwork. They are
copied unmodified from https://github.com/KoG-teeworlds/brand_and_media (path brand/logo/) at commit
700f64d29bca1413a0c1fbab435852a5cc2e47c7. scripts/fetch_brand.py fetches them from that commit and checks their
SHA-256; a test fails if a file differs.
